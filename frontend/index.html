The Challenge

Migrant Workers Face Fragmented Healthcare

When migrant workers move between districts, their medical history doesn't always follow them — causing gaps in care.

📂

Scattered Records

Medical history spread across paper records, different hospitals, and multiple locations.

🌐

Language Barriers

Difficulty communicating health needs in an unfamiliar language in a new location.

🗺️

Unfamiliar Places

Workers don't know where the nearest hospital, clinic, or pharmacy is located.

Solution

Everything a Migrant Worker Needs

🔐

Secure Health Records

Portable, encrypted digital health profile accessible anywhere in Kerala. Role-based access ensures only authorized healthcare providers can view records.

JWT Auth Encrypted Role-based
🌐

Multilingual AI Assistant

Chat in English, Tamil, Malayalam, or Hindi to get healthcare help instantly.

📍

Nearby Healthcare

Find hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies near your current location instantly.

🚨

Emergency QR Card

Scan-ready emergency card with blood group, allergies, and emergency contacts.

💊

Medicine Reminders

Set reminders for prescribed medicines so you never miss a dose.

🏥

Provider Dashboard

Authorized healthcare providers can view records and add consultation notes.

📋

Audit Trail

Every record access is logged for transparency and accountability.
